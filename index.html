Section 02

Audience, tone and key messages

The reader is an engineer choosing a BMS IC, so the page leads with numbers and gets to products and the form fast.

Primary
BMS and hardware design engineers at OEMs and battery pack makers, from high-end equipment to cost-sensitive designs.
Secondary
Engineering managers, product managers and procurement evaluating a BMS supplier; distributors; press; job candidates.
Tone
Confident, precise, technical. Numbers instead of adjectives. Short sentences. No hype words the data does not back up.

Key messages, in priority order

  1. Accuracy. 1 mV per cell with no calibration, 0.02% coulomb-counting repeatability, 100 µΩ impedance resolution.
  2. Battery health in one click. NovaEIS™ measures impedance in-circuit, with no external excitation and no lab equipment.
  3. Simplicity. A complete BMS on one IC plus the Nova BMS Suite turns months of work into hours.
  4. Sustainability. Better balancing and health data keep batteries in service longer and reduce waste.

Tab 1

DAA Technology

The architecture behind the accuracy. new

Digitally Assisted Analog is Nova's patented architecture. Every cell gets its own dedicated ADC and reference, and all cells and current are sampled at the same moment. That precision is what makes protection, balancing, fuel gauging and NovaEIS™ possible on one IC — without the complexity and cost traditionally attached to this level of performance. edited

Graphic: cells → dedicated ADC + reference per cell → DAA core → Protection · Balancing · Fuel Gauging · NovaEIS™.

Tab 2

NovaEIS™

Understanding batteries, not just monitoring them. original

Voltage, current, and temperature reveal how a battery is operating. Impedance reveals how healthy it is. NovaEIS™ provides real-time insight into battery condition, degradation, and State of Health through direct per-cell impedance measurement with repeatable 100 µΩ resolution, bringing practical battery intelligence directly into real-world battery systems. original

Graphic: healthy vs unhealthy cell — same voltage · same temperature · same current. Caption: “Battery Intelligence reveals the difference. NovaEIS™ delivers the insight you can't see.”

Tab 3

Precision Measurement

1 mV was just the beginning. original

DAA-powered precision measurement delivers 1 mV accuracy, 0.02% coulomb-counting repeatability, simultaneous measurement, and dedicated ADC and reference resources for every cell. This architecture provides the consistency and precision required for advanced battery management functions. original

Graphic: iceberg — “1 mV accuracy” above water; NovaEIS™, Fuel Gauging, Diagnostics, Complete BMS, Simultaneous Measurement, Dedicated ADC & Ref. per Cell below. Caption: “Precision measurement enabled by DAA powers everything that follows.”

Tab 4

Fuel Gauging

Know exactly how much energy remains. original

Accurate fuel gauging should not be about guessing. It should be about precise measurement. Accurate State-of-Charge (SOC) estimation starts with precise measurement of the charge flowing into and out of the battery. Nova's PureCoulomb™ fuel gauging delivers industry-leading 0.02% coulomb-counting repeatability, enabling highly accurate fuel gauging and reliable runtime prediction throughout the life of the battery. edited

Graphic: clean SOC gauge with state of charge in % and remaining runtime. Replaces the cartoon water tank.

Tab 5

Protection & Balancing

A healthy battery is a protected and balanced battery. original

Lithium-ion batteries are highly sensitive to overvoltage, undervoltage, overcurrent, and temperature extremes. At the same time, manufacturing tolerances, aging, and operating conditions cause cells to drift apart over time. Nova's simultaneous cell measurement and 1 mV accuracy enable precise protection thresholds and effective balancing decisions, helping maximize safety, capacity, and battery life throughout the life of the pack. edited

Graphic: a chain of cells with one weak link highlighted. Caption: “A battery pack is only as strong as its weakest link.”

Tab 6

Complete BMS

More than monitoring. Complete battery management. original

Traditional battery management systems often require developers to assemble multiple functions from separate hardware, firmware, and software components. This increases complexity, extends development time, and introduces additional points of failure. Nova integrates precision measurement, protection, balancing, diagnostics, and NovaEIS™ battery intelligence into one streamlined platform — reducing development effort while improving performance, reliability, and scalability. edited

Graphic: six puzzle pieces (NovaEIS™, Precision Measurement, Fuel Gauging, Protection, Balancing, Diagnostics & Communications) forming one base: Complete BMS — NB1600. Link: “Compare the ICs” → Products.

Section 12

Functionality and visual direction

All text is live HTML, every call to action leads to the same form, and the look moves from a site-builder template to a clean engineering brand.

Functionality

  • One page with anchor navigation, smooth scroll and an active menu state.
  • Every CTA opens the single form with request type (and product, where relevant) preselected.
  • Form submissions go to info@novasemi.com; workshop requests are tagged separately. CRM integration if the client has one.
  • Tabs switch without page reload; vertical tabs and product tabs become accordions under 768 px.
  • Schematics open in a lightbox with zoom.
  • White papers download as PDF (direct or behind the form — to confirm).
  • No text baked into images: timeline, iceberg, cell comparison, chain, puzzle and EIS comparison are rebuilt as HTML or SVG, readable on mobile and indexable.
  • SEO: page title, meta description, Open Graph image, schema.org Organization and Product markup for the four ICs.
  • Analytics events: CTA clicks, form submits by request type, PDF downloads, tab opens in Products.
  • Performance: WebP images and SVG graphics, Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 s on mobile.

Visual direction

Colors
The logo blue as the main brand color, deep neutral backgrounds, a cool white for text. A second accent used only in gradients and the neon timeline.
Typography
One sans-serif family for all text, with a light weight and a gradient for accent words in headlines and tabular figures for numbers.
Text layout
Left-aligned body text, 60–75 characters per line. No centered paragraphs with scattered bold words.
Graphics
All infographics redrawn as clean vector illustrations in brand colors, one consistent line and icon style.
Imagery
Product renders, the NB1400 evaluation board photo and Nova BMS Suite screenshots. No generic stock photos.
Overall feel
Precise, light, technical — closer to a modern chip company than to a template site.
